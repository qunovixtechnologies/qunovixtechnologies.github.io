Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Local-first task management: To Do List stores your tasks and preferences primarily on your device. Task data leaves your device when you choose a feature such as Google Drive backup, file export, sharing, or speech processing. Separate advertising, purchase, account-authorization, and technical data may be processed by the third-party services described below.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how To Do List (the "App"), provided by Qunovix Technologies ("we," "our," or "us"), handles information when you use the App.

This policy applies to the App and its integrated services. Third-party services have their own privacy policies and act under their own terms.

2. Information Stored Locally

The App may store the following information on your device:

  • Task information: Task titles, task lists, completion status, due dates, reminder dates, recurrence, priority, progress, flags, moods, creation dates, and deletion dates.
  • Recycle-bin information: Deleted tasks and the date each task was deleted.
  • Settings and preferences: Theme, language, date and time format, notification preferences, vibration level, voice-alert settings, day-summary settings, and related App choices.
  • Premium status: A local record that an eligible Premium purchase has been recognized.
  • Advertising frequency information: A locally saved timestamp and an in-memory transition count used to avoid showing full-screen ads too frequently.
  • Widget information: Task counts and task details needed to display optional home-screen widgets.
  • Backup data: JSON backup files created or restored at your request.

We do not operate an App backend that stores or reads your task database. Local information remains on your device unless you use an optional transfer or third-party feature described below.

3. Task Visibility on Your Device

If you enable notifications, widgets, or spoken alerts, task information may be visible or audible outside the App. For example, a task title may appear on your lock screen, in a notification, or in a home-screen widget, or may be spoken aloud using text-to-speech.

You can manage notification visibility, widgets, permissions, and text-to-speech behavior through the App and your device settings.

4. Voice Entry and Text-to-Speech

When you choose voice entry, the App uses the speech-recognition service available on your device and receives the resulting recognized text. Depending on your device and speech-service provider, audio or related technical information may be processed on the device or sent to that provider. The App does not intentionally save a recording of your voice.

When voice alerts are enabled, task titles may be sent to your device's text-to-speech service so they can be spoken. Speech recognition and text-to-speech providers process data under their own privacy policies.

5. Backups, Imports, Exports, and Sharing

5.1 Local backup and restore

When you create a local backup, the App generates a JSON file containing task data, custom lists, and relevant settings. You choose the storage location through the system file picker. Anyone who can access an unencrypted backup file may be able to read its contents.

When you restore a backup, the App reads the file you select and imports supported information into local App storage.

5.2 Google Drive backup and restore

If you choose Google Drive backup or restore, the App uses Google Sign-In and requests access to the Google Drive application-data folder. The App uploads and downloads To Do List backup files in that private application-data area of your Google Drive account.

The App uses Google account authorization only to provide the requested backup or restore operation. We do not operate our own server for storing your Google access token or Drive backup. Google's handling of your account and files is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

To Do List's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

5.3 Shared text and external sharing

The App can receive text you deliberately share from another app and can open your device's sharing interface when you choose to share App-related content. The apps, services, or people you select may receive and independently process that information.

6. Advertising

Unless Premium is active, the App may display banner, native, interstitial, or other advertising supplied through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its advertising partners may automatically process information such as:

  • Advertising or app-instance identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from network information;
  • Device model, language, operating system, App version, and network information;
  • Ad requests, impressions, clicks, interactions, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention signals.

Depending on your region, device settings, Google account settings, and the advertising choices available to you, ads may be personalized, non-personalized, or limited. Learn more from Google's advertising information and Google's Privacy Policy.

The App stores a limited timestamp locally to enforce a minimum interval between full-screen ads. Advertising is disabled in the App while an active Premium entitlement is recognized.

7. Premium Purchase and Google Play Billing

To Do List Premium is offered as a one-time, non-consumable purchase that removes advertising. Purchases are processed by Google Play. Google may collect and process payment information, account information, order history, tax information, and fraud-prevention signals under its policies.

The App receives purchase-related information needed to unlock or restore Premium, such as the product identifier, purchase status, purchase token, and verification data. The App does not receive or store your complete payment-card details.

8. Permissions and Device Access

The App may request or use the following permissions and device capabilities:

  • Internet and network state: Used for ads, Google Play Billing, Google Sign-In, Google Drive operations, and external links.
  • Microphone: Used only when you start voice entry.
  • Notifications: Used for task reminders, task updates, daily summaries, status information, and notification actions.
  • Vibration: Used for optional reminder and feedback patterns.
  • Alarms and boot events: Used to schedule reminders and restore eligible schedules after a restart or App update.
  • Files: Accessed through the system file picker when you choose a backup or restore location.
  • Sharing and browser services: Used only when you choose to share content or open an external link.

You can manage permissions through your device settings. Disabling access may limit the corresponding feature.

9. How Information Is Used

Information is used to:

  • Create, organize, display, search, complete, restore, and permanently delete tasks;
  • Provide reminders, recurrence, day summaries, widgets, statistics, speech input, and spoken alerts;
  • Save and restore your choices and task data;
  • Create, upload, download, import, or restore backups when you request those actions;
  • Display and measure advertising in the free version;
  • Process, recognize, and restore Premium purchases through Google Play;
  • Prevent fraud, maintain security, troubleshoot errors, and comply with applicable law.

10. Information Sharing

We do not sell your task content. Information may be processed or disclosed only in the following circumstances:

  • At your direction: When you create a backup, use Google Drive, import or share text, open a link, or select another app or person to receive information.
  • Service providers: Google may process information for AdMob, Google Play Billing, Google Sign-In, and Google Drive. Your device or chosen service provider may process information for speech recognition, text-to-speech, notifications, alarms, file selection, or sharing.
  • Legal and safety reasons: When disclosure is reasonably necessary to comply with law, legal process, enforce our rights, prevent fraud, or protect users, the public, or services.
  • Business changes: If the App or business is reorganized, sold, or transferred, relevant information under our control may be transferred subject to this Policy and applicable law. Because task data is primarily local, we generally do not possess that local content to transfer.

11. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Active tasks: Remain on your device until you delete them, clear App data, or uninstall the App.
  • Recycle bin: Deleted tasks are retained locally for up to 15 days unless you restore or permanently delete them sooner. After 15 days, the App automatically removes them permanently.
  • Preferences and Premium status: Remain locally until App data is cleared or the App is uninstalled, subject to restoration or verification through the relevant platform.
  • Local backup files: Remain where you saved them until you delete them.
  • Google Drive backups: Remain in your Google Drive application-data storage until deleted through Google account controls, App functionality where available, or Google's retention processes.
  • Advertising and purchase information: Is retained by Google and its partners under their respective policies.

To remove local App data, delete tasks and empty the recycle bin, clear the App's storage through device settings, or uninstall the App. Clearing local data does not automatically delete backup files or information held by third parties.

12. Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to the App's local-first design and limit the information sent to third parties to what is needed for the feature you use. However, no device, app, network, file, account, or storage system is completely secure.

Protect your device and Google account, review lock-screen notification settings, and store backup files in a secure location. Local and Google Drive backups are not end-to-end encrypted by Qunovix Technologies.

13. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or request portability of personal information and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Because task data is primarily stored locally, you can exercise many choices directly by editing or deleting tasks, permanently deleting recycle-bin items, changing permissions and notification settings, deleting backup files, clearing App data, or uninstalling the App.

Advertising choices may also be available through your Google account, device settings, or controls presented by Google. For information controlled by Google or another provider, submit requests directly to that provider. You may contact us using the details below for information under our control.

14. Children's Privacy

The App is a general task-management tool and is not directed specifically to children under 13. We do not knowingly operate a server that collects task content from children. Parents or guardians should supervise a child's use, manage device permissions and advertising settings, and decide whether backup, sharing, voice, and notification features are appropriate.

If you believe we hold personal information about a child that should be deleted, contact us. Local App data can be removed from the child's device as described above.

15. International Processing

Google and other third-party providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Their privacy policies describe their processing locations and transfer safeguards. Where applicable, we handle information under relevant data-protection requirements.

16. External Links
