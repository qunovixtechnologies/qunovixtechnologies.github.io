2. The Game

Arrow Nest is an arrow puzzle game: tap arrows to send them off the board in the order that lets every arrow escape. It works offline. Depending on the version, it may include:

  • a campaign of levels with increasing difficulty and picture boards, a Daily Challenge with medals and monthly trophies, and the monthly Koi Pond Rush event;
  • a Weekly League in which you compete for points against computer-simulated rivals, with promotion between leagues and hint rewards;
  • lives, hints, stars, scores, streaks, profile avatars and frames, and colour themes;
  • optional rewarded ads that give hints or extra lives, and interstitial ads between levels; and
  • optional in-app purchases: Remove Ads and hint packs.

The Game does not include chat, multiplayer, real-player leaderboards, user-generated content, or real-money prizes. Levels, daily puzzles, events, league rules, rewards, prices, and game rules may be adjusted over time to keep the Game balanced.

We cannot recover lost progress or Virtual Items, other than helping you restore Remove Ads.

The Weekly League runs on your device. Your opponents are computer-simulated rivals whose scores are generated by the Game; they are not real people. League weeks run from Monday to Monday in your device's local time. Koi Pond Rush events run for a calendar month and their progress resets when a new event begins. Rewards for leagues and events are Virtual Items, are given only as described in the Game, and may change between seasons.
