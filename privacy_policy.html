Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

1. Who We Are

Qunovix Technologies ("Qunovix," "we," "our," or "us") provides the Financial Calculator India mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App and its service providers collect, why it is used, how it is protected, and the choices available to you.

2. Information We Process

2.1 Calculator and Local App Data

The App does not require an account, name, email address, or phone number for its core calculator features. Calculator inputs and results are calculated on your device. Saved results, display preferences, language, rates, purchase status, and your Firebase report-sharing choice are stored locally on your device using Hive or the relevant app-store service.

2.2 Optional Firebase Report Data

Firebase report sharing is optional and off unless you affirmatively allow it. Before the first upload, the App displays an in-app disclosure describing the data and asks for your permission. You can later turn this setting on or off under Settings > Help improve free reports.

If you allow Firebase report sharing, the App sends the following to Google Firebase Cloud Firestore when you view, save, share, or export a calculator report:

  • The report action: viewed, saved, shared, or PDF exported
  • The calculator type and report labels
  • All values, inputs represented in the report, calculation results, metrics, tables, timelines, and report notes
  • The report creation time and a server-recorded upload time
  • The App package name, version, and build number

We use this information only to operate, monitor, troubleshoot, and improve the free report service and App features. We do not intentionally send your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or Firebase Authentication account because the App does not request or create such an account.

When Firebase processes a request, Google may also process technical service data such as IP address, Firebase app and project identifiers, installation or device-related identifiers used by the SDK, and operational logs. Google processes this data as described in its service terms and privacy materials.

2.3 Advertising and Google Play Data

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and Google's configuration, AdMob may process advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location inferred from an IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic data to provide, measure, prevent fraud in, and personalize or limit ads.

Google Play Services processes information needed for in-app purchases, purchase restoration, in-app reviews, and app updates. Payment information is handled by Google Play and is not provided directly to us.

2.4 Rate Synchronization

When the App synchronizes public bank interest-rate information, it makes an internet request to the configured public data source. The source or hosting provider may receive standard network information such as your IP address and request metadata. Calculator values and saved reports are not included in this request.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide calculations, saved results, reports, and requested App features
  • Operate, monitor, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the optional free report service
  • Display and measure advertisements through Google AdMob
  • Process purchases and provide Google Play features
  • Prevent misuse and comply with legal obligations

4. Service Providers and Disclosure

We use service providers to perform specific functions:

  • Google Firebase Cloud Firestore: optional storage and processing of report events and report contents
  • Google AdMob: advertising delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention
  • Google Play Services and Google Play: billing, purchase restoration, reviews, and updates

These Google services are subject to Google's Privacy Policy. Additional Firebase privacy and security information is available from Firebase.

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect users or the App, or as part of a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

5. Consent and Your Choices

  • Firebase report sharing: Choose "Not now" in the disclosure to prevent report uploads. You can enable or disable future uploads at any time in the App's Settings. Disabling it does not delete reports already uploaded.
  • Advertising: Use any consent controls shown by the App or Google and your Android advertising privacy settings to manage personalized advertising where available.
  • Local information: Delete individual saved results in the App or clear the App's storage/uninstall the App to remove locally stored App data.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Local settings and saved results remain on your device until you delete them, clear App storage, or uninstall the App. Optional Firebase reports are retained only for as long as reasonably needed to operate, monitor, troubleshoot, and improve the service, meet legal obligations, and prevent abuse, after which they are deleted or anonymized.

Because Firebase reports are not linked to an App account, the App cannot automatically locate them by account. You may request access to or deletion of Firebase report data by emailing us. To help us locate a report, include the calculator type, approximate date and time, action, App version, and any distinctive report values you are comfortable providing. We may retain information when required by law or for legitimate security and fraud-prevention purposes.

7. Security and International Processing

Firebase report data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We use Firebase project permissions, administrative access controls, and Firestore security rules designed to prevent unauthorized client access to report documents. No electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Google and other service providers may process information in countries other than yours. Their processing is governed by their applicable terms, data-protection commitments, and legal safeguards.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum digital-consent age in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided report data, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

9. Permissions

The App uses internet access to load advertisements, synchronize public rates, access Google Play features, and—only after you opt in—send report data to Firebase.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection concerning your personal information, and to withdraw consent. You may also complain to an appropriate data-protection authority. Contact us to make a request.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our providers, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy here and update the date above. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request new consent.
