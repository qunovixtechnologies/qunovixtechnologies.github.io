Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

1. Who We Are

Qunovix Technologies ("Qunovix," "we," "our," or "us") provides the Financial Calculator India mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App and its service providers collect, why it is used, how it is protected, and the choices available to you.

2. Information We Process

2.1 Calculator and Local App Data

The App does not require an account, name, email address, or phone number for its core calculator features. Calculator inputs and results are calculated on your device. Saved results, display preferences, language, rates, and purchase status are stored locally on your device using Hive or the relevant app-store service.

The App does not upload your calculator inputs, results, saved reports, or usage events to us or to any server operated by us, other than the crash reports described in section 2.4. Reports you share or export are sent only to the destination you choose.

2.2 Advertising and Google Play Data

The App uses Google AdMob to display ads. Depending on your region, consent choices, device settings, and Google's configuration, AdMob may process advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location inferred from an IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic data to provide, measure, prevent fraud in, and personalize or limit ads.

Google Play Services processes information needed for in-app purchases, purchase restoration, in-app reviews, and app updates. Payment information is handled by Google Play and is not provided directly to us.

2.3 Rate Synchronization

When the App synchronizes public bank interest-rate information, it makes an internet request to the configured public data source. The source or hosting provider may receive standard network information such as your IP address and request metadata. Calculator values and saved reports are not included in this request.

2.4 Crash Reports

If the App crashes or encounters a serious error, it sends a crash report to Google Firebase Crashlytics. A crash report may include the error and stack trace, the App version and build number, device model, operating system version, available memory and storage, device orientation, the time of the crash, and a Firebase installation identifier. Crash reports do not include your calculator inputs, results, saved reports, name, email address, or phone number.

We use crash reports only to diagnose, fix, and prevent problems in the App. We do not use them for advertising and do not combine them with information that identifies you.

3. How We Use Information

  • Provide calculations, saved results, reports, and requested App features
  • Diagnose and fix crashes and errors using crash reports
  • Display and measure advertisements through Google AdMob
  • Process purchases and provide Google Play features
  • Prevent misuse and comply with legal obligations

4. Service Providers and Disclosure

We use service providers to perform specific functions:

  • Google Firebase Crashlytics: crash reporting and diagnostics
  • Google AdMob: advertising delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention
  • Google Play Services and Google Play: billing, purchase restoration, reviews, and updates

These Google services are subject to Google's Privacy Policy. Additional Firebase privacy and security information is available from Firebase.

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information if required by law, to protect users or the App, or as part of a business transfer subject to appropriate safeguards.

5. Consent and Your Choices

  • Advertising: Use any consent controls shown by the App or Google and your Android advertising privacy settings to manage personalized advertising where available.
  • Local information: Delete individual saved results in the App or clear the App's storage/uninstall the App to remove locally stored App data.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Local settings and saved results remain on your device until you delete them, clear App storage, or uninstall the App. Crash reports are retained by Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days and then deleted. Information processed by service providers such as Google AdMob and Google Play is retained under their own policies.

Because the App has no accounts and we do not store your calculator data on our servers, we generally hold no information that identifies you. You may contact us by email with any privacy question or request. We may retain information when required by law or for legitimate security and fraud-prevention purposes.

7. Security and International Processing

Network requests made by the App, such as rate synchronization, crash reporting, advertising, and Google Play requests, use encrypted connections where supported by the provider. Data stored locally is protected by your device's security. No electronic system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Google and other service providers may process information in countries other than yours. Their processing is governed by their applicable terms, data-protection commitments, and legal safeguards.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum digital-consent age in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where appropriate.

9. Permissions

The App uses internet access to load advertisements, synchronize public rates, send crash reports, and access Google Play features.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection concerning your personal information, and to withdraw consent. You may also complain to an appropriate data-protection authority. Contact us to make a request.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, our providers, or legal requirements change. We will post the revised policy here and update the date above. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request new consent.
