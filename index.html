Flagship · Lifestyle

My DiaryJournal with Lock

A private place for thoughts, moods and the moments worth keeping.

Write about today, or any day. Lock it with a PIN, a pattern or your fingerprint. Add a mood to each entry and look back at how your weeks felt. Entries stay on your device, and you can write offline.

  • PIN, pattern & fingerprint lock
  • Mood tracking & statistics
  • Works fully offline
  • Write for any past date
  • Stored locally by default
  • Optional Google Drive backup

Productivity

To Do ListTasks & Reminders

Plan the day, get the nudge, tick it off.

Keep work, home, shopping and wishlists in separate lists. Set reminders with the sound, voice or vibration you like. Save birthdays so you don't forget them. Deleted something by mistake? It stays in the Recycle Bin for 15 days.

  • Lists for every part of life
  • Voice, sound & vibration reminders
  • Birthday reminders
  • 15-day Recycle Bin
  • Light & dark themes
  • Local & Google Drive backup

Health & Fitness

WeightTrackWeight Tracker

See how far you've come, one weigh-in at a time.

Log each weigh-in with a note and, if you like, a progress photo. WeightTrack turns them into clear trends, weekly averages and streaks. It works whether your goal is to lose, gain or maintain.

  • Kilograms or pounds
  • Goals, BMI & body-fat estimate
  • Trends, averages & streaks
  • Progress photos
  • Health Connect import
  • Widget, reminders & CSV export

(02) — The Promise

Your phone. Your data.

We build every app around a few simple rules. They shape what we build, and what we leave out.

Local-first

Your entries, tasks and records are stored on your device by default.

Backups on your terms

Cloud backup is optional. It only runs if you turn it on.

Useful on the first tap

No long setup and nothing to learn. Open the app and start.

Always improving

We read every review and ship updates often.

The best app is the one that gets out of your way and lets you get on with your day.
