Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Local-first health tracking: WeightTrack stores profiles, weight records, notes, and progress-photo references locally on your device. Qunovix Technologies does not operate a server that receives or stores this content. Information may leave your device only when you request a feature such as sharing an export, importing Health data, viewing an ad, or completing a Google Play purchase.

1. Who We Are

WeightTrack (the "App") is provided by Qunovix Technologies ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the App.

2. Information Stored Locally

The App may store the following information on your device:

  • Profile information: profile name, sex, age, height, starting weight, target weight, unit preference, theme choice, and active-profile status;
  • Weight records: weight, date and time, optional notes, and whether a record is used as the starting weight;
  • Progress photos: photos you take or select and the local file paths associated with weight records;
  • Preferences: language, reminder settings, notification identifiers, theme settings, and home-widget data;
  • Purchase entitlement: a local flag indicating that Google Play confirmed or restored the one-time Remove Ads purchase; and
  • Ad controls: consent state managed by Google's User Messaging Platform and limited frequency information used to avoid showing interstitial ads too often.

This information remains on your device unless you export, back up, share, or otherwise transmit it using a feature you select.

3. Health Connect and HealthKit

If you choose the health-import feature, WeightTrack requests permission to read body-weight samples from Android Health Connect or Apple HealthKit. The App reads the latest available weight sample from a limited recent period and saves the imported value as a local WeightTrack record. WeightTrack does not write data back to those services and does not use health data for advertising.

Health access is optional. You can deny or revoke it in your device settings. Data obtained from Health Connect or HealthKit is used only to provide the health-import and weight-tracking features you request and is not sold or shared with advertising providers.

4. Exports, Backups, and Sharing

You can create and share CSV exports or JSON backups. These files may contain profile details, weight records, dates, notes, settings, and local photo-path references. JSON backups do not include the photo files themselves. Files are created only when you request them and are sent to the location or app you choose through your device's sharing or file interface.

You are responsible for protecting exported files and for deleting copies from email, cloud storage, messaging apps, or other destinations when they are no longer needed.

5. Advertising and Consent

The free version may display banner and interstitial advertisements using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its advertising partners may process advertising identifiers, IP-derived approximate location, device and app information, consent signals, ad impressions and interactions, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention signals. WeightTrack does not send your entered weight, profile, notes, progress photos, or imported health records to AdMob for ad targeting.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform requests advertising privacy choices before the App requests ads. If a privacy-options entry is required, it appears in Settings so you can review or change those choices. Depending on your region and choices, Google may serve personalized, non-personalized, or limited ads.

Learn more from Google's Privacy Policy, How Google uses information for advertising, and AdMob policies.

6. Google Play Billing and Remove Ads

WeightTrack offers a one-time, non-consumable Remove Ads product through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the transaction and payment method. We do not receive your full payment-card or bank-account details.

The App receives product information, localized price, purchase status, purchase token or verification data, and restoration results needed to start, verify, acknowledge, restore, and deliver the ad-free entitlement. A local entitlement flag is stored after a successful or restored purchase. Google processes purchase information under the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.

7. Permissions

Depending on the features you use, WeightTrack may request:

  • Camera and photos/media: to take, select, save, or share progress photos;
  • Health weight read access: to import a weight sample when you request it;
  • Notifications, alarms, and boot completion: to schedule optional daily reminders and restore them after a device restart;
  • Files/storage: to import or export backups and share CSV files where required by the operating system; and
  • Internet: for ads, consent messages, Google Play Billing, reviews, and external links.

You can manage permissions in device settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the related feature from working.

8. How Information Is Used

Information is used to:

  • provide profiles, weight history, charts, statistics, goals, reminders, progress photos, and the home widget;
  • import weight data and create exports or backups when you request those actions;
  • display and measure ads for users who have not purchased Remove Ads;
  • process, acknowledge, restore, and remember the Remove Ads entitlement;
  • maintain security, prevent fraud and invalid advertising activity, diagnose problems, and comply with law and platform rules.

9. Sharing and Third-Party Processing

We do not sell your locally stored weight or health information. Information may be processed by:

  • Google Mobile Ads and consent partners for advertising, consent, measurement, and fraud prevention;
  • Google Play for product details, payment processing, purchase verification, restoration, refunds, and order management;
  • Health Connect or HealthKit when you request health import;
  • your operating system and apps you choose for notifications, files, photos, exports, backups, reviews, or sharing; and
  • authorities or other parties when required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.

10. Retention and Deletion

Local data remains until you delete records or profiles, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App. Progress-photo files may need to be removed separately if a copy exists outside the App. Exported files remain in the destination you selected until you delete them.

Purchase and transaction records are retained by Google Play according to Google's policies and legal obligations. Uninstalling the App does not cancel, refund, or erase a Google Play order. A valid Remove Ads purchase can be restored using the Google Play account that owns it.

11. Security

We use reasonable safeguards appropriate to a local-first app, but no device, network, file, or software system is completely secure. Protect your device with a screen lock, keep the operating system updated, and store exports or backups securely.

12. Children's Privacy

WeightTrack is not directed to children under 13. A parent or guardian should supervise any minor's use and decide whether health, photo, reminder, advertising, and purchase features are appropriate. Do not allow a child to make a purchase without authorization from the Google Play account holder.

13. Your Choices and Rights

You can edit or delete local records, remove profiles, revoke permissions, turn reminders off, change advertising privacy choices where available, purchase Remove Ads, clear App data, or uninstall the App. Depending on your location, you may also have legal rights concerning personal information processed by us or third parties. Contact us to make a request; for information controlled by Google, use Google's privacy and account tools.

14. International Processing

Google and other device or platform providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their policies describe the safeguards they use for international transfers.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to WeightTrack, third-party services, laws, or platform requirements. The current version will show its latest revision date.
