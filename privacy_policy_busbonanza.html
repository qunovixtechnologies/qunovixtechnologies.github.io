Bus Bonanza for Android

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 16 November 2026

Privacy summary: Bus Bonanza is an offline-first bus parking puzzle game. Your progress, coins, boosters, and settings are saved on your Android device, and there is no Bus Bonanza account or game server. The Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the Game is played and to fix crashes; you can turn both off in Settings ▸ Share Usage & Crash Data. It shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may collect device identifiers and usage data when ads are requested and shown. Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play, and we never see your payment details. Where the law requires it, you can review your ad choices in Settings ▸ Privacy Choices. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Scope and Developer

This Privacy Policy explains how Bus Bonanza (the “Game”), Android package com.qunovixtechnologies.busbonanza, handles information. The Game is provided by Qunovix Technologies (“we,” “us,” or “our”), located in Pune, Maharashtra, India. This Policy applies to the Game and not to third-party apps, websites, advertisers, or services that you reach through it, including websites opened by tapping an advertisement.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

To save your game, Bus Bonanza keeps a save file in the Game's private storage on your device. It contains:

  • game progress: the levels you have reached, stars earned per level, wins, win streak and best streak, and the level you are currently playing so you can resume it;
  • your in-game coin balance and boosters (VIP, Arrange, and Remix), and your available Spin Wheel spins;
  • daily reward timing: which Daily Bonus day you are on and when Supplies can next be claimed;
  • settings: music, sound, and vibration, and which tutorial and one-time tips you have already seen;
  • simple counters used to limit how often ads appear, such as levels completed since the last ad; and
  • a record of purchases made in the Game (product ID and the transaction identifier returned by Google Play) so each purchase is granted only once and can be restored.

The save file does not contain your name, email address, or phone number, and it is not sent to us or to anyone else.

3. Analytics and Crash Reporting (Google Firebase)

Bus Bonanza uses two Firebase services provided by Google LLC to see how the Game is played and to find and fix problems:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase receives gameplay events — for example levels started, completed, or failed (with level number and boosters used), coins earned and spent, rewards claimed, rewarded ads requested, and screens viewed — together with a Firebase app-instance ID, device model, Android version, language, app version, approximate location derived from IP address, and session information. If you allow it, Analytics may also use the Android advertising ID; your ad consent choices are passed to Analytics as well.
  • Firebase Crashlytics receives crash and error reports, which contain the stack trace, device model and state (such as free memory and orientation), Android and app version, a Crashlytics installation ID, and a short log of recent game events and the screen and level you were on.

These reports do not include your name, email address, or the contents of your save file. We use them only in aggregate to improve levels and difficulty and to fix crashes, and Google processes them under the Firebase privacy and security terms. You can turn off both services at any time in Settings ▸ Share Usage & Crash Data; from then on no analytics events or crash reports are sent. Google Analytics data is kept for 14 months and Crashlytics crash reports for 90 days.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Bus Bonanza is free to play and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. The Game shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one (for example, to get extra coins, boosters, spins, or parking spots), and may show a limited number of interstitial ads between levels after you finish a level, never while you are solving it. Interstitial ads start only after the early levels and are spaced out. Buying “Remove Ads” stops interstitial ads; rewarded ads remain optional.

When ads are requested and shown, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information such as:

  • the device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) or, where that is unavailable, other device or app-set identifiers;
  • IP address, from which an approximate location (such as country or city) may be derived;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating-system version, language, and app version;
  • ad interactions, such as ads shown, clicked, or completed; and
  • diagnostic and performance information related to ad delivery.

Google uses this information to deliver, measure, and improve ads, prevent fraud and abuse, and — where allowed — to show ads based on your interests. On supported Android versions, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may also use Android's privacy-preserving advertising features (such as ad attribution and topics). We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob (such as the number of ads shown and earnings); we do not receive your advertising ID from Google. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Ad Privacy and Consent

Bus Bonanza uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage ad consent where it is legally required:

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland (and other places where Google requires it): Google's consent form is shown, and ads are requested only once your consent status allows it. You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy Choices, which appears in Settings whenever these privacy options apply to you.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android device's Google or Privacy settings.

Google's consent tools store your consent preferences on your device.

6. In-App Purchases

Optional purchases — such as Remove Ads and packs of coins or boosters — are processed by Google Play Billing. Payment details are collected and handled by Google under its own terms and privacy policy; Qunovix Technologies never sees your card number or other payment credentials. Google provides the Game with the information needed to confirm and deliver a purchase, such as the product ID, purchase status, and a transaction identifier. Google Play may also provide us with order and transaction reports through the Play Console. We use this information only to deliver, restore, and support purchases, handle refunds, and meet legal and accounting obligations.

7. What We Do Not Collect

Bus Bonanza does not:

  • require or offer an account, sign-in, or profile;
  • see or store your card number or other payment credentials;
  • have chat, multiplayer, online leaderboards, or user-generated content; or
  • access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, or phone calls.
No Bus Bonanza servers or account: Apart from the Google services described in this Policy, Bus Bonanza does not send data to any server, and the puzzles work without an internet connection. Unity's own analytics, ads, and diagnostics services are turned off.

8. Contacting Support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include, such as your device model or the level you have reached. We use it only to answer you and fix problems, and delete it when it is no longer needed.

9. Android Permissions and Device Features

  • Internet and network state: used to send analytics events and crash reports, load ads, show the consent form, and process purchases. The puzzles themselves work without a connection.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) and Android ad services: declared by the Google Mobile Ads SDK so ads can be delivered and measured as described above.
  • Google Play Billing: used to offer and process in-app purchases.
  • Vibration: used for optional haptic feedback, for example when a bus departs or a move is blocked. You can turn it off in Settings ▸ Vibration.
  • Wake lock and foreground service: may be declared by Google's libraries so background tasks, such as finishing an ad or purchase request or sending a report, can complete reliably.
  • Device clock: the Game reads your device's date and time to time Daily Bonus and Supplies rewards. This stays on your device.

Bus Bonanza does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, or phone calls.

10. How Information Is Used and Shared

We and the services described above use information to:

  • save and restore your game progress and settings on your device;
  • understand how the Game is played, balance level difficulty, and find and fix crashes (Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, unless you turn them off);
  • serve rewarded and interstitial ads and respect your ad privacy choices;
  • deliver, restore, and support in-app purchases;
  • answer support requests; and
  • keep the Game working, secure, and free from fraud and abuse.

Information is shared only with Google as our analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, payment, and distribution provider, as described in this Policy, or where disclosure is required by applicable law or necessary to protect rights, safety, or security. We do not sell or rent personal information. Because your save file stays on your device, we cannot view or disclose it.

11. Retention and Deletion

  • Your save file remains on your device until you clear the Game's storage in Android settings or uninstall Bus Bonanza.
  • Depending on your Android settings, Android's own device backup may include the Game's data in your Google account backup and restore it on a new device. Manage this in your device's backup settings.
  • Remove Ads is linked to your Google Play account and can be restored after reinstalling through Settings ▸ Restore Purchases. Coin and booster packs are consumable and are restored only from a device backup.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase event data is kept for 14 months and Crashlytics crash reports for 90 days, after which they are deleted.
  • Information collected by Google for ads and purchases is kept according to Google's retention policies.

12. Security

Game data is kept in Android's private app storage, which other apps cannot normally read, and communication with Google's analytics, crash-reporting, ad, and billing services uses encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your device secure.

13. Children

Bus Bonanza is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information through the Game, contact us and we will help, including by explaining how to turn off analytics, limit personalised ads, and remove the Game's data from the device.

14. International Processing

Google and its partners may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States, under their own safeguards and privacy policies.

15. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You can:

  • review or change your ad consent in Settings ▸ Privacy Choices, where it applies;
  • reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android device settings;
  • buy Remove Ads to stop interstitial ads;
  • turn off analytics and crash reporting in Settings ▸ Share Usage & Crash Data;
  • turn off music, sound, and vibration in the Game's Settings; and
  • delete your game data by clearing the Game's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the Game.

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR, UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we do not hold your save data or ad identifiers, most requests can be fulfilled with the controls above. For information held by Google, use your Google Account controls. You may also contact us about any information we directly hold, such as purchase records and support emails.
