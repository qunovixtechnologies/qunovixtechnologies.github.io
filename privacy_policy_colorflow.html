Color Flow – Water Sort Puzzle

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 8 October 2026

Privacy summary: Color Flow is an offline-first water sort puzzle game. Your progress, diamonds, settings, and purchase records are saved on your device. There is no Color Flow account and Qunovix Technologies does not run a game server that receives your save data. The game uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the game is played and to fix crashes, shows ads through Google AdMob, and processes in-app purchases through Google Play. These Google services may collect device identifiers, usage data, and diagnostics. Where the law requires it, Google's consent form lets you manage your ad choices, and you can reopen it from Settings ▸ Ad privacy. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Scope and Developer

This Privacy Policy explains how Color Flow – Water Sort Puzzle (the “Game”), Android package com.qunovixtechnologies.colorflow, handles information. The Game is provided by Qunovix Technologies (“we,” “us,” or “our”), located in Pune, Maharashtra, India. This Policy applies to the Game and not to third-party apps, websites, advertisers, or services that you reach through it, including websites opened by tapping an advertisement.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

To save your game, Color Flow keeps a save file (with a backup copy) in the Game's private storage on your device. It may contain:

  • game progress: campaign level progress, stars, best scores, daily puzzle results and streaks, lucky spin status, milestones, and statistics;
  • in-game balances and items: diamonds, unlocked themes and bottle cosmetics, and hints or other helpers;
  • settings: sound, music, vibration, reduced motion, high contrast, large text, colour symbols, and tutorial status;
  • simple counters used to limit how often ads appear, such as the number of levels completed since the last ad; and
  • a record of purchases made in the Game (product ID and the purchase token or order reference returned by Google Play) so each purchase is granted only once and can be restored.

Color Flow does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location, and it does not require you to create an account. The save file is not sent to a Qunovix Technologies server.

3. Analytics and Crash Reports (Firebase)

Color Flow uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, services of Google LLC, to understand how the Game is used, balance its difficulty and rewards, and find and fix problems.

  • Google Analytics for Firebase records game events, such as starting a session, starting and completing a level, rewarded ad offers and completions, and purchase results. It also collects the app-instance ID, device model, operating-system version, language, app version, approximate location derived from the IP address, and session information such as first open and engagement time.
  • Firebase Crashlytics collects crash and error reports when the Game stops working, including stack traces, device model, operating-system version, free memory and storage, device orientation, app version, and a Crashlytics installation identifier.

This information is not used to identify you by name. We use it only in aggregated or diagnostic form to improve the Game. Learn more in the Firebase privacy information.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Color Flow is free to play and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. The Game shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one (for example, to add a bottle, get free diamonds, double a level reward, skip a level, or double a spin reward) and a limited number of interstitial ads between levels, only after you have completed several levels and with limits on how often they appear. Interstitial ads are not shown during play. Buying “Remove Ads” stops interstitial ads; rewarded ads remain optional.

When ads are requested and shown, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information such as:

  • the device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) or, where that is unavailable, other device or app-set identifiers;
  • IP address, from which an approximate location (such as country or city) may be derived;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating-system version, language, and app version;
  • ad interactions, such as ads shown, clicked, or completed; and
  • diagnostic and performance information related to ad delivery.

Google uses this information to deliver, measure, and improve ads, prevent fraud and abuse, and — where permitted — to show ads based on your interests. We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob (such as the number of ads shown and earnings); we do not receive your advertising ID from Google. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Ad Privacy and Consent

Color Flow uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage ad consent where it is legally required:

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom, Switzerland, and other regions where required: Google's consent form is shown and ads are requested according to the choice you make. Where Google requires an in-app way to change that choice, an Ad privacy button appears in Settings so you can review or change it at any time.
  • Other regions: ads are served under Google's standard rules, and you can limit personalisation through your Android device settings.

Google's consent tools may store your consent preferences on your device. You can additionally reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android device's Google or Privacy settings.

6. In-App Purchases

Optional purchases, such as diamond packs, Remove Ads, theme and bottle bundles, and the Ultimate bundle, are processed by Google Play Billing. Payment details are collected and handled by Google under its own terms and privacy policy; Qunovix Technologies never sees your card number or other payment credentials. Google provides the Game with information needed to confirm and deliver a purchase, such as the product ID, purchase status, and a purchase token. Google Play may also provide us with order and transaction reports through the Play Console. We use this information only to deliver, restore, and support purchases, handle refunds, and meet legal and accounting obligations.

7. Android Permissions and Device Features

  • Internet and network state: used to send analytics events and crash reports, load ads, show the consent form, and process purchases. The puzzles themselves work without a connection; analytics events are sent later when a connection is available.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID): declared by the Google Mobile Ads SDK so ads can be delivered and measured as described above.
  • Vibration: used for optional haptic feedback. You can turn it off in the Game's Settings.
  • Google Play Billing: used to offer and process in-app purchases.

Color Flow does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, or phone calls.

8. How Information Is Used and Shared

We and the services described above use information to:

  • save and restore your game progress and settings;
  • understand how the Game is played, balance difficulty and rewards, and find and fix crashes and bugs;
  • serve rewarded and interstitial ads and respect your ad consent choices;
  • deliver, restore, and support in-app purchases; and
  • keep the Game working, secure, and free from fraud and abuse.

Information is shared only with Google as our analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, and payment provider, as described in this Policy, or where disclosure is required by applicable law or necessary to protect rights, safety, or security. We do not sell or rent personal information. Because your save file stays on your device, we generally cannot view or disclose it.

No Color Flow servers or account: Apart from the Google services described in this Policy, Color Flow does not send data to any server. It has no leaderboard or cloud-save service. If a future version adds services of this kind, we will update this Policy before they are used.

9. Retention and Deletion

  • Your save file remains on your device until you clear the Game's storage or uninstall Color Flow. There is no server account for us to delete.
  • Depending on your Android settings, Android's own device backup may include app data in your Google account backup. Manage this in your device's backup settings.
  • Non-consumable purchases (Remove Ads and the bundles) are linked to your Google Play account and can be restored after reinstalling. Diamonds and other progress are not restored unless a device backup restores them.
  • Google Analytics event-level data is kept for the retention period set in our Google Analytics settings and then deleted automatically. Crashlytics crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.
  • Information collected by Google for ads and purchases is kept according to Google's retention policies.

10. Security

Game data is kept in Android's private app storage, which other apps cannot normally read, and communication with Google's analytics, crash-reporting, ad, and billing services uses encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your device and Google account secure.

11. Children

Color Flow is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information through the Game, contact us and we will help address it, including by explaining how to limit ad personalisation and remove the Game's data from the device.

12. International Processing

Google and its partners may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States, under their own safeguards and privacy policies. Your save file remains on your device.

13. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You can:

  • review or change your ad consent through Settings ▸ Ad privacy where it is available;
  • reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android device settings;
  • ask us to delete analytics data linked to your device's app-instance ID by contacting us (see below);
  • buy Remove Ads to stop interstitial ads;
  • turn off vibration, sound, and music in the Game's Settings; and
  • delete all game data by clearing the Game's storage in Android settings or uninstalling the Game.

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR, UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we do not hold your save data or ad identifiers, most requests can be fulfilled with the controls above. Analytics and crash data are pseudonymous and cannot be linked to your name, so to help with a request about them we may ask you for details from your device. For information held by Google, use your Google Account controls. You may also contact us about any information we directly hold, such as purchase records.
