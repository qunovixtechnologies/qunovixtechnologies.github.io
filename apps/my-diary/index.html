(05) — FAQ

Good questions.

Can't find your answer? Email qunovixtechnologies@gmail.com.

Is My Diary private?

Yes. Your entries are stored locally on your device by default, and you can lock the diary with a password or with your fingerprint on supported devices.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. You can write and read your journal completely offline.

What happens when I change phones?

Create a backup, either locally or with Google Drive, and restore it on your new device.

Can I export my diary?

Yes. You can export your entries as PDF or text files.

What if I delete an entry by mistake?

Deleted entries go to the recycle bin, so you can recover them.
