(05) — FAQ

Good questions.

Can't find your answer? Email qunovixtechnologies@gmail.com.

Is To Do List free?

Yes, it's free to download. An optional one-time Premium purchase removes advertisements.

Can reminders speak to me?

Yes. Reminders can use a custom sound, a voice or vibration.

Can I get deleted tasks back?

Yes. Deleted records stay in the Recycle Bin for 15 days so you can restore them.

Is there a dark mode?

Yes. You can switch between light and dark themes.

Can I back up my tasks?

Yes. You can use local backup or Google Drive backup.
