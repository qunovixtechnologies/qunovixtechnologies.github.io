Every tap counts. Find the right order and clear the maze.

(04) — FAQ

Good questions.

Can't find your answer? Email qunovixtechnologies@gmail.com.

How do I play?

Tap any arrow to move it in the direction it points. Arrows can't pass through each other, so plan the order. Clear every arrow to finish the level.

What if I get stuck?

Use a hint to see a way forward on tricky mazes.

Is there a time limit?

No. There are no timers, so you can play at your own pace.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Amaze Blast works without an internet connection.
