(04) — FAQ

Good questions.

Can't find your answer? Email qunovixtechnologies@gmail.com.

What age is it for?

KidsEnglish is designed for young learners who are starting to recognise English letters, numbers and everyday words.

What can children learn?

The alphabet, numbers and counting, fruits and vegetables, animals, birds and insects, colours and shapes, body parts, months, weekdays and seasons, transport, sports, festivals, jobs and more.

Are there games?

Yes. Basic maths challenges, jigsaw puzzles, slide puzzles and Tic Tac Toe, plus drawing and colouring.

Can parents change settings?

Yes. A parents-only area, protected by a number check, holds settings such as the child's name, sound and progress reset.
