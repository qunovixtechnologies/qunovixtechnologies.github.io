(04) — FAQ

Good questions.

Can't find your answer? Email qunovixtechnologies@gmail.com.

Do I need the internet to play?

No. Every mode works offline. Ads and purchases need a connection.

How many levels are there?

Journey has 1,000 levels across five worlds: Blooming Garden, Crystal Ocean, Starlight Valley, Golden Temple and Aurora Peaks. Classic is endless, and there's a new Daily Challenge every day.

When does a game end?

When none of the pieces in your tray fit on the board, so plan ahead.

Can I remove ads?

Yes. A one-time in-app purchase removes ads between games. Reward ads are always your choice.

Does it work on tablets?

Yes. Block Trails is designed for portrait play on phones and tablets.
