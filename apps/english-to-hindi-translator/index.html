(04) — FAQ

Good questions.

Can't find your answer? Email qunovixtechnologies@gmail.com.

Which languages does it support?

English and Hindi, in both directions.

Can I translate text from a photo?

Yes. Use the camera to capture printed text such as signs, menus or documents. The app recognises English and Devanagari text and translates it.

How does conversation mode work?

The English and Hindi speakers each get their own microphone. Translated messages appear in a chat layout, so both people can follow along.

Can the app read translations aloud?

Yes. Text-to-speech reads the translation, and you can adjust the voice speed.
