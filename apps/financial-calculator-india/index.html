(06) — FAQ

Good questions.

Can't find your answer? Email qunovixtechnologies@gmail.com.

Is the app free?

Yes. Financial Calculator India is free to download.

Which calculators are included?

More than 40, across banking, post office schemes, mutual funds, retirement, tax, insurance, bonds and general tools. See the full list above.

Does the income tax calculator support the new regime?

Yes. It supports both the old and the new tax regime.

Are FD and RD rates up to date?

The app can sync the latest publicly available FD/RD rates for supported banks.

Can I save or share a result?

Yes. Save results on your device, share them through your phone's share options, or export supported reports as PDF.

Please note: The calculators are for guidance, education and planning only. Results depend on the inputs, assumptions, rates and rules used.
