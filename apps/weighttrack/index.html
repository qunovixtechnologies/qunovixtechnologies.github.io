(05) — FAQ

Good questions.

Can't find your answer? Email qunovixtechnologies@gmail.com.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. WeightTrack is designed as a local-first tracker, so your records, notes and progress photos stay on your phone.

Do I need Health Connect?

No. Health Connect access is optional and only used when you ask to import a weight.

Can more than one person use it?

Yes. You can create multiple profiles on one device.

Can I export my weight history?

Yes. You can export it as a CSV file, and create backups that include profiles, settings, records and photos.

Does it support kilograms and pounds?

Yes, you can record your weight in either.

Please note: WeightTrack provides general tracking and informational tools and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.
