(05) — FAQ

Good questions.

Can't find your answer? Email qunovixtechnologies@gmail.com.

Do I need an account?

No. BMI Calculator works without any account or registration.

What's the difference between WHO and DGE?

They use the same ranges from normal weight upward. WHO splits underweight into three levels, while DGE uses a single underweight category below 18.5.

Which units can I use?

Kilograms and centimetres, pounds with feet and inches, or stones. Values convert automatically when you switch.

Is BMI right for everyone?

BMI is a general screening measurement based on height and weight. Standard adult categories may not suit children, adolescents, pregnant people, older adults, athletes, or people with certain medical conditions or body compositions.

Is my information uploaded?

No. Your inputs and preferences are stored on your device and are not uploaded to Qunovix Technologies.

Please note: BMI does not directly measure body fat, muscle mass, bone density, fitness, nutrition or overall health. BMI Calculator is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, monitoring or treatment.
