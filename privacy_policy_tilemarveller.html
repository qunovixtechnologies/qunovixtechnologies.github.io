Tile Marveller for Android

Privacy Policy

Effective: 26 October 2026

Tile Marveller ("the Game") is published by Qunovix Technologies ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the Game handles, who receives it and the choices you have.

1. Information stored on your device

Your level progress, coins, boosters, journey scenes, settings and current board are stored on your device. We do not operate user accounts or game servers, and we do not receive this information. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Analytics and crash reporting

The Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, provided by Google. Analytics receives gameplay events (such as levels started, completed or failed, boosters used, purchases started, screens viewed and rewarded videos requested) together with an app-instance ID, device model, Android and app version, language and approximate location derived from your IP address. Crashlytics receives crash and error reports with the stack trace, device state, an installation ID and a short log of recent game events. These reports do not include your name, email address or save file. We use them only to understand how the Game is played, improve it and fix crashes. You can turn both off at any time in Settings > Share Usage & Crash Data. Google keeps Analytics data for 14 months and crash reports for 90 days.

3. Advertising

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), we ask for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy Choices, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
Google's practices are described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive only the purchase confirmation needed to deliver your item, not your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to Google Play transactions.

5. Google sign-in and cloud backup (Save Your Progress)

If you choose Sign in with Google, the Game uses Google's sign-in (OAuth) to receive your Google account name and email address, which it shows so you know which account is connected, and permission to store one file containing your game progress in the private app-data folder of your Google Drive. This folder is hidden from you and from other apps, and the Game cannot see any other files in your Drive. Your progress is synced automatically while you are signed in. Access tokens are short-lived and are not stored by the Game, and we do not receive or keep your account details or backup on our own servers. You can sign out or delete the cloud backup at any time in Settings > Save Your Progress, and you can remove the Game's access in your Google Account settings at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions
The Game's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Backup files

You can also save your progress to a file through the Android file picker and restore it later. The file goes only to the location you choose, and restoring reads only the file you pick.

7. Vibration

The Game uses your device's vibration motor for gameplay feedback. You can switch it off in Settings.

8. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Data retention and security

We do not keep gameplay data on our own servers. Firebase keeps analytics data for 14 months and crash reports for 90 days. Your cloud backup stays in your Google Drive until you delete it. Other providers retain data according to their own policies. Data sent by the Game to Google services is encrypted in transit.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Use the in-game settings described above, or contact us and we will respond within the time required by law.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version, and material changes will be shown in the Game, where you will be asked to accept them.
