HabitLoop for Android

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 25 August 2026

Privacy summary: HabitLoop is an offline-first habit and life tracker. Your habits, tasks, moods, money records, focus sessions, journal entries, and attachments are stored primarily in the App's private storage on your Android device. Qunovix Technologies does not operate a HabitLoop account or tracker-data server, does not sell your information, and does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. Network features are limited and are explained below.

1. Scope and Developer

This Privacy Policy explains how HabitLoop (the “App”), Android package com.qunovixtechnologies.habit_loop, handles information. The App is provided by Qunovix Technologies (“we,” “us,” or “our”), located in Pune, Maharashtra, India. This Policy applies to HabitLoop, not to third-party apps, websites, device features, app stores, or services that you choose to use with it.

2. Information You Create and Store

Depending on the features you use, HabitLoop may store the following in its private database and files on your device:

  • Habits and tasks: names, descriptions, categories, icons, colours, schedules, targets, checklist items, priorities, reminders, notes, completion status, progress values, and history;
  • Mood: check-in date and time, mood level, tags, notes, and custom mood-tag settings;
  • Money: account names and types, currencies, opening and calculated balances, income, expenses, transfers, categories, budgets, dates, notes, and optional receipt-image paths;
  • Focus: categories, goals, planned and actual durations, start and end times, completion status, and optional links to habits;
  • Journal: titles, rich-text content, dates, mood labels, tags, photos, voice notes, audio, and other files you attach;
  • App settings: theme, language, navigation, sorting, sound, notification, app-lock, currency, exchange-rate, widget, onboarding, and Pro-entitlement preferences; and
  • Technical app data: locally generated record identifiers, notification identifiers, database metadata, backup metadata, and widget snapshots needed to operate the App.

HabitLoop uses this information to provide the tracker, reminders, widgets, statistics, correlations, charts, search, focus timer, journal, money tools, export, backup, security, personalisation, and Pro features you request. Qunovix Technologies does not receive your local tracker database merely because you use the App.

3. Photos, Files, Receipts, and Voice Notes

When you choose a photo or file, Android's picker gives HabitLoop access to the item you selected. Journal attachments are copied into the App's private documents storage. A money receipt may be referenced from the selected location. If you record a journal voice note, microphone audio is recorded only while you activate the recording control and is saved as an audio attachment on your device. HabitLoop does not continuously listen to the microphone.

Source files, copies retained by a document provider, shared copies, and exported or backed-up copies are controlled separately. Removing an attachment or record from HabitLoop may not delete those other copies.

4. Information We Do Not Collect on Our Servers

HabitLoop does not require a Qunovix Technologies account and does not include third-party advertising, behavioural analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not sell, rent, or use your tracker content for advertising, data brokerage, credit decisions, or training general-purpose artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models. The App does not request contacts, call logs, SMS, or device location permissions.

Google Play, Android, Google Drive, exchange-rate hosts, websites, email apps, and sharing destinations may independently process technical, account, transaction, or content information under their own privacy policies when you use those services.

5. Limited Online Services

HabitLoop uses the internet only for features that need it:

  • Exchange rates: automatic exchange-rate updates are enabled by default and may run periodically in the background. The App requests a public daily currency file from jsDelivr or a Cloudflare Pages mirror. The request includes the base currency code in the URL and a HabitLoop/1.0 user-agent. Like an ordinary internet request, the host can receive network information such as your IP address, request time, and connection metadata. No habit, mood, journal, focus, transaction, account-balance, or attachment content is sent. You can turn automatic rates off in Settings and enter rates manually.
  • Google Play Billing: if you view, buy, or restore HabitLoop Pro, Google Play processes product, price, payment, account, device, and transaction information. HabitLoop receives purchase-status information needed to unlock and restore Pro and stores the entitlement state locally. Qunovix Technologies does not receive your full payment-card details through the App.
  • App-store delivery: Google Play and Android may process device, installation, update, diagnostics, security, and usage information independently as part of distributing and operating apps.

Google processes its services under the Google Privacy Policy. The exchange-rate dataset is provided by the Exchange API project through its hosting providers.

6. Optional Google Drive Backup and Restore

Google Drive backup is optional and available only when you choose it. HabitLoop uses Google Sign-In to authenticate you and requests only the Google Drive application-data scope. This scope lets the App create, find, replace, download, and restore its own backup file, habitloop-backup.hloop, in the connected Google account's hidden appDataFolder. It does not let HabitLoop browse your ordinary Drive files.

The backup can contain all tracker database records and referenced journal or receipt media. Before upload, the App compresses and encrypts the backup on your device using AES-256-GCM with a key derived from the password you choose using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256. Your backup password is not uploaded inside the backup and cannot be recovered by us. Google still processes account, authorization, file, device, and network metadata, and retains the encrypted file under its own practices. Qunovix Technologies does not receive the backup or your Google password.

Google API Limited Use disclosure: HabitLoop's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the user-facing Drive backup and restore feature. It is not sold, used for advertising or credit decisions, transferred to data brokers, or used to train general-purpose AI or ML models.

You can revoke HabitLoop's Google access from your Google Account settings. Revoking access stops future API access but may not delete an existing backup. Manage or delete Google-held app data using the controls Google makes available. Restoring a backup replaces the current HabitLoop tracker dataset after confirmation.

7. Android Platform Backup and Device Transfer

Separately from HabitLoop's password-protected backup feature, Android may back up or transfer eligible App data through the device's operating-system backup and restore features, depending on your Android version, Google account, device manufacturer, and settings. Such platform backup is controlled by Android or your device provider and may include private App files and preferences. Review your device's backup settings if you do not want Android to make those copies. Platform backup is governed by the relevant provider's privacy and retention practices and is not operated by Qunovix Technologies.

8. User-Directed Sharing and External Destinations

HabitLoop sends information outside the App only when you choose an action or enable a network feature. Examples include:

  • sharing an encrypted local backup through the Android share sheet;
  • sharing a CSV export of money transactions;
  • opening the Play Store, an exchange-rate project page, device-help site, or another external link; and
  • opening your email app to send suggestions or support messages.

You select the destination and recipient. Once content is sent to an email app, messaging app, cloud provider, file manager, browser, or other destination, that provider's terms and privacy policy apply. Review the content and recipient before sharing sensitive journal, mood, habit, or financial information. If you contact us, we receive the email address, message, attachments, and other information you voluntarily provide and use it to respond, troubleshoot, and maintain necessary support records.

9. Android Permissions and Device Features

  • Internet: used for optional Drive backup and restore, Google Play Billing, exchange-rate updates, and online actions you request.
  • Notifications and exact alarms: used to display habit and task reminders, timer status, and completion alerts at the times you configure, subject to Android approval and power-management limits.
  • Receive boot completed: lets the notification component restore scheduled reminders after a restart or App update.
  • Microphone: used only when you record a journal voice note.
  • Biometric and fingerprint capability: used only when you enable App lock.
  • Foreground service: keeps a user-started Pomodoro focus countdown running and visible through a persistent Android notification.
  • Photo and document pickers: provide access to only the photo, receipt, backup, or other file you select through Android's picker interface.
  • Home-screen widgets: store and display limited tracker summaries, such as progress, streak, mood, money, or focus information, in Android's widget storage.

App lock invokes Android's system authentication screen and receives an authentication result. HabitLoop does not receive or store your PIN, pattern, password, fingerprint, face image, or biometric template. App lock is an access convenience, not encryption of the local database, attachments, notifications, widgets, exports, or backups.

10. How Information Is Disclosed

We do not disclose your local tracker content because we do not receive it, except if you voluntarily send it to us. Information otherwise leaves the App only at your direction, through the online services described above, through Android platform backup or transfer settings, or if we are legally required to disclose information that is actually in our possession. We may also use information you send us when reasonably necessary to protect users, rights, safety, and security or to investigate abuse.

11. Retention and Deletion

  • Local records and preferences remain until you edit or delete them, clear the App's storage, or uninstall HabitLoop. Archived habits remain until restored or permanently deleted.
  • Journal media copied by HabitLoop is removed when the corresponding attachment or journal entry is permanently deleted, where the file is still accessible. Source files and outside copies are not affected.
  • Local password-protected backups remain in the App's documents area or any destination to which you share or copy them. When a new local export is created, HabitLoop retains up to seven App-created backup files in its backup folder.
  • The encrypted Drive backup remains in your Google account until it is replaced or deleted, subject to Google's controls and retention practices.
  • CSV exports, shared files, email messages, and files saved by other apps remain wherever you or the receiving provider stores them until deleted there.
  • Google Play retains purchase and transaction information under its legal and operational requirements. HabitLoop keeps a local Pro-entitlement flag so purchased features remain available.
  • Android platform backups or device-transfer copies remain under the platform provider's settings and retention practices.

Clearing HabitLoop's storage or uninstalling it removes its active local data from that device, but does not automatically delete Drive backups, Android platform backups, source files, Play purchase history, exported files, shared copies, or messages already sent. Because there is no Qunovix Technologies tracker account, we generally cannot remotely view, recover, correct, or delete data that remains only on your device or in your personal cloud account.

12. Security

HabitLoop uses Android private application storage, authenticated encryption for App-created backup files, encrypted network connections supplied by Android and service providers, and an optional system-authentication App lock. Local database and media files are not separately encrypted by HabitLoop and rely on Android and device security. No system is completely secure. Anyone who can unlock your device, view a widget or notification, access your Google account, learn your backup password, or access an export or share destination may be able to view corresponding information. Use a strong device lock and a unique backup password, keep devices updated, and protect exported files and connected accounts.

13. Sensitive Information and Your Responsibilities

HabitLoop can hold private journal, wellbeing, routine, and financial information. You decide what to record and are responsible for having the right to store any information about another person. Avoid entering information you do not need or are not authorised to use. HabitLoop is not a medical record system, bank, accounting service, or emergency service.

14. Children's Privacy

HabitLoop is a general-audience productivity app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's tracker content on a Qunovix Technologies server. A parent or guardian who allows a child to use the App should supervise the child's entries, sharing, purchases, and use of Google services. If you believe a child has sent personal information directly to us through support, contact us so we can review and, where appropriate, delete it.

15. Your Choices and Rights

You can:

  • use the core App without creating a Qunovix Technologies account;
  • review, edit, archive, or delete supported records within the App;
  • deny or revoke microphone, notification, exact-alarm, and biometric access in Android settings, understanding that related features may stop working;
  • turn off automatic exchange-rate updates and maintain rates manually;
  • choose whether to create, share, upload, or restore a backup;
  • revoke Google Drive access through your Google Account and manage data held by Google;
  • manage subscriptions and purchase history through Google Play; and
  • clear App storage or uninstall HabitLoop to remove active local App data from the device.

Depending on where you live, privacy law may give you additional rights regarding personal information that we actually hold, such as support correspondence. Email us to make a request. We may need to verify your request and may retain information when required by law or for legitimate security, dispute, and recordkeeping needs. For data held by Google or another provider, submit the request to that provider.

16. International Processing

Your local tracker data remains on your device unless you use backup, sharing, Android transfer, or another online feature. Google, hosting providers, email services, and share destinations may process information in countries other than your own, subject to their policies and applicable safeguards.
