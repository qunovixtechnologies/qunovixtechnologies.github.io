Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 16 November 2026

Privacy summary: Word Atlas is a word search game. Your progress, coins, hints, profile, and settings are saved on your Android device, and there is no Word Atlas account. Qunovix Technologies does not run a game server that receives your save data. The Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to see how it is played and to fix crashes. These Google services may collect device identifiers and usage data. Where the law requires it, you can review your ad choices in Settings ▸ Privacy Choices. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Scope and Developer

This Privacy Policy explains how Word Atlas (the “Game”), Android package com.qunovixtechnologies.wordatlas, handles information. The Game is provided by Qunovix Technologies (“we,” “us,” or “our”), located in Pune, Maharashtra, India. This Policy applies to the Game only. It does not cover third-party apps, websites, advertisers, or services that you reach through it, including websites opened by tapping an ad.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

To save your game, Word Atlas keeps a save file (and a backup copy of it) in the Game's private storage on your device. It may contain:

  • puzzle progress: campaign levels and destinations completed, the words found and hinted on boards in progress, Daily Challenge and crossword progress, and collected destination stamps and quotes;
  • your daily activity: current and highest Daily Streak, the date you last played, the Daily Reward you opened, and Daily Wisdom task progress and badges;
  • your coin balance and your hints and bursts;
  • your profile: a player name (“Explorer” by default, which you can change in Settings ▸ Profile) and the avatar you pick from the Game's built-in set;
  • settings: music, sound, puzzle language, your analytics and crash-reporting choice, and which tutorials you have already seen;
  • simple counters used to limit how often ads appear, such as levels completed since the last ad; and
  • a record of purchases made in the Game (the product ID and the transaction identifier from Google Play), so that each purchase is delivered only once and Remove Ads can be restored.

Word Atlas does not ask for your phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location, and it does not require you to create an account. The save file itself is not sent to us or to anyone else. The player name you choose is used only on your device and is not included in analytics or crash reports.

3. Analytics and Crash Reporting (Google Firebase)

Word Atlas uses two Firebase services provided by Google LLC to see how the Game is played and to find and fix problems:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase receives gameplay events. Examples include levels and daily puzzles started, completed, or left (with level number, mode, and language); hints, bursts, and coins used or earned; rewarded ads requested; purchases made; screens viewed; and settings changed. Each event comes with a Firebase app-instance ID, device model, Android version, language, app version, approximate location derived from your IP address, and session information. If you allow it, Analytics may also use the Android advertising ID. Your ad consent choices from Google's consent form are passed to Analytics as well.
  • Firebase Crashlytics receives crash and error reports. A report contains the stack trace, the device model and state (such as free memory and orientation), the Android and app version, a Crashlytics installation ID, and a short log of recent game events, including the screen and level you were on.

These reports do not include your email address, your player name, or the contents of your save file. We use them only in aggregate, to improve puzzles and difficulty and to fix crashes. Google processes them under the Firebase privacy and security terms. You can turn off both services at any time in Settings ▸ Share Usage & Crash Data. After that, no analytics events or crash reports are sent. Google Analytics data is kept for 14 months and Crashlytics crash reports for 90 days.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Word Atlas is free to play and is supported by ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. The Game may show:

  • rewarded ads, only when you choose to watch one, for example to earn coins, hints, or bursts;
  • interstitial ads, full-screen ads shown between levels after you finish a puzzle, never while you are solving one, and limited in how often they appear; and
  • banner ads, small ads shown in part of the screen.

Buying Remove Ads stops interstitial and banner ads. Rewarded ads remain available as an optional way to earn rewards.

When ads are requested and shown, Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information such as:

  • the device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID) or, where that is unavailable, other device or app-set identifiers;
  • IP address, from which an approximate location (such as country or city) may be derived;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating-system version, language, and app version;
  • ad interactions, such as ads shown, clicked, or completed; and
  • diagnostic and performance information related to ad delivery.

Google uses this information to deliver, measure, and improve ads and to prevent fraud and abuse. Where allowed, it also uses it to show ads based on your interests. On supported Android versions, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may also use Android's privacy-preserving advertising features (such as ad attribution and topics). We receive only aggregated reports from AdMob, such as the number of ads shown and earnings. We do not receive your advertising ID from Google. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Ad Privacy and Consent

Word Atlas uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage ad consent where it is legally required:

  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland (and other places where Google requires it): Google's consent form is shown, and ads are requested only once your consent status allows it. You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings ▸ Privacy Choices, which appears in Settings whenever these privacy options apply to you.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android device's Google or Privacy settings.

Google's consent tools store your consent preferences on your device.

6. In-App Purchases

Optional purchases are Remove Ads, coin packs, and hint and burst packs. Google Play Billing processes them. Google collects and handles your payment details under its own terms and privacy policy, and Qunovix Technologies never sees your card number or other payment credentials. Google gives the Game the information it needs to confirm and deliver a purchase, such as the product ID, purchase status, and a transaction identifier. Google Play may also give us order and transaction reports through the Play Console. We use this information only to deliver, restore, and support purchases, to handle refunds, and to meet legal and accounting obligations.

7. Contacting Us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include, such as your device model or app version. We use it only to answer you and fix problems, and we delete it when it is no longer needed.

8. Android Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used to load ads, show the consent form, process purchases, and send analytics events and crash reports. All puzzles are included in the app and can be played without a connection.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) and Android ad services: declared by the Google Mobile Ads SDK so that ads can be delivered and measured as described above.
  • Google Play Billing: used to offer and process in-app purchases.
  • Wake lock and foreground service: declared by Google's libraries so that background tasks, such as finishing an ad or purchase request, can complete reliably.

Word Atlas does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, or phone calls.

9. How Information Is Used and Shared

We, and the services described above, use information to:

  • save and restore your game progress and settings on your device;
  • understand how the Game is played, balance puzzle difficulty, and find and fix crashes (Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics, unless you turn them off);
  • serve rewarded, interstitial, and banner ads and respect your ad privacy choices;
  • deliver, restore, and support in-app purchases;
  • answer support requests; and
  • keep the Game working and secure, and protect it from fraud and abuse.

We share information only with Google, as our analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, and payment provider, as described in this Policy, or where applicable law requires disclosure or it is necessary to protect rights, safety, or security. We do not sell or rent personal information. Because your save file stays on your device, we cannot view or disclose it.

No Word Atlas servers or account: Apart from the Google services described in this Policy, Word Atlas does not send data to any server. It has no account, cloud save, chat, multiplayer, or online leaderboard, and Unity's own analytics and diagnostics services are turned off.

10. Retention and Deletion

  • Your save file stays on your device until you clear the Game's storage in Android settings or uninstall the Game. Both permanently delete your progress, coins, hints, profile, and settings.
  • Depending on your device settings, Android's own backup may include the Game's data in your Google account backup. You can manage this in your device's backup settings.
  • Remove Ads is linked to your Google Play account and can be restored after reinstalling through Settings ▸ Restore Purchases. Coin, hint, and burst packs are consumable and are restored only if a device backup restores your balance.
  • Support emails are kept only as long as needed to resolve your request.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase event data is kept for 14 months and Crashlytics crash reports for 90 days. After that, they are deleted.
  • Information that Google collects for ads and purchases is kept according to Google's retention policies.

11. Security

Game data is kept in Android's private app storage, which other apps cannot normally read. Communication with Google's analytics, crash-reporting, ad, and billing services uses encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your device and Google account secure.

12. Children

Word Atlas is a general-audience word puzzle game. It is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information through the Game, contact us. We will help, for example by explaining how to turn off analytics, limit personalised ads, and remove the Game's data from the device. You can also use Google Play's parental controls and purchase authentication to control purchases.

13. International Processing

Google and its partners may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States, under their own safeguards and privacy policies.

14. Your Choices and Privacy Rights

You can:

  • turn off analytics and crash reporting in Settings ▸ Share Usage & Crash Data;
  • review or change your ad consent in Settings ▸ Privacy Choices, where it applies;
  • reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your Android device settings;
  • buy Remove Ads to stop interstitial and banner ads;
  • change your player name and avatar in Settings ▸ Profile, and turn music and sound on or off in Settings; and
  • delete your game data by clearing the Game's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the Game.

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR, UK GDPR, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your personal information, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we do not hold your save data or ad identifiers, the controls above handle most requests. For information held by Google, use your Google Account controls. You may also contact us about information we hold directly, such as purchase records and support emails.
