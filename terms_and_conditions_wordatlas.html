2. The Game

Word Atlas is a word search puzzle game with a travel theme. You find the hidden words in each letter grid by swiping across them. All puzzles are included in the app and can be played offline. Depending on the version, the Game may include:

  • a campaign of word search levels grouped into destinations, with a stamp collected for each completed destination;
  • a Daily Challenge with a quotation to collect, an archive of past daily puzzles, and a Daily Streak;
  • a Daily Reward gift and Daily Wisdom tasks with badges;
  • crossword puzzles in the “More puzzles” section;
  • coins, hints, and bursts that help you find words, plus a player name and avatar;
  • puzzles in several languages, selected in Settings ▸ Select Language, and an in-game Help centre;
  • rewarded ads that give coins, hints, or bursts, plus interstitial and banner ads; and
  • optional in-app purchases: Remove Ads, coin packs, and hint and burst packs.

The Game has no chat, multiplayer, online leaderboards, user-generated content, or real-money prizes. Puzzles, destinations, quotations, rewards, prices, and game rules may be added, corrected, or rebalanced in updates.
