2. What LensDesk Provides

LensDesk is an offline-first photographer booking calendar and business-record tool. Depending on the App version and configuration, features may include:

  • a colour-coded calendar, date availability, daily agenda, and booking-conflict warnings;
  • booking, client, venue, package, team, note, status, payment, balance, and reminder records;
  • custom event types, revenue summaries, search, CSV and PDF export, and availability sharing;
  • generated booking-confirmation and payment-reminder messages;
  • a 30-day recycle bin for deleted bookings;
  • optional device-credential app lock;
  • optional one-way Google Calendar event synchronisation; and
  • optional manual or automatic Google Drive app-data backup and restore.

LensDesk does not provide a Qunovix Technologies user account, hosted booking marketplace, client portal, payment-processing service, photography-file storage service, or guarantee that a client will attend or pay.
