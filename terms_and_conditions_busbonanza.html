2. The Game

Bus Bonanza is a bus parking puzzle game: tap buses to drive them out of a crowded lot in the right order and board the waiting passengers before you run out of spots. It works offline. Depending on the version, it may include:

  • a campaign of levels with increasing difficulty, stars, wins, and win streaks;
  • in-game coins earned by completing levels and claiming rewards;
  • boosters (VIP, Arrange, and Remix) that can be earned or bought with in-game coins;
  • a Shop where boosters are exchanged for in-game coins;
  • optional in-app purchases, such as Remove Ads and packs of coins or boosters;
  • a Daily Bonus, timed free Supplies, and a Spin Wheel that awards coins or boosters;
  • optional rewarded ads that give extra coins, boosters, spins, or parking spots, and interstitial ads between levels; and
  • music, sound effects, and optional vibration.

The Game does not include accounts, chat, multiplayer, online leaderboards, user-generated content, or real-money prizes. Levels, rewards, coin and purchase prices, booster rules, and game rules may be adjusted over time to keep the Game balanced.
