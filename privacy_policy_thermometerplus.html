Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Privacy summary: Room Temperature Thermometer, displayed on the device as Thermometer Plus, does not require an account. App settings, the selected location, cached weather, and up to 50 weather-history entries are stored locally on your device. Qunovix Technologies does not operate an app backend that receives this local content. The App does connect directly to weather, map, advertising, font, and Google Play services as described below.

1. Who We Are and Scope

Room Temperature Thermometer / Thermometer Plus (the "App") is provided by Qunovix Technologies ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you install or use the App.

This Policy applies to the App. Third-party services have their own privacy policies and are responsible for their own processing.

2. Information Handled by the App

2.1 Location information

If you choose Use Current Location, the App requests approximate or precise location from the operating system while the App is in use. The App does not request background location. Location access is optional, and you can instead select a place manually on the map.

The selected place, latitude, longitude, location label, and whether the place was selected manually are stored locally so the App can restore your selection. Coordinates are sent to weather services to request local conditions and forecasts. Requests to the primary weather service use coordinates rounded to two decimal places. A fallback weather request may use coordinates rounded to four decimal places. When converting a device position into a place name, the reverse-geocoding request may include the position at greater precision.

2.2 Weather information and history

The App receives temperature, apparent or feels-like temperature, humidity, pressure, wind, forecast, sunrise, sunset, precipitation probability, location label, and request time from weather providers. The latest reading and up to 50 recent outdoor-weather readings are stored locally to provide History and a saved reading when live data is temporarily unavailable.

2.3 Indoor sensor and battery information

The App checks for an ambient-temperature sensor. If one is unavailable, it may use battery-temperature information to calculate an indoor estimate. It also reads charging status to explain when device heat may affect the estimate. These raw sensor and charging readings are processed on the device and are not intentionally sent by the App to weather providers or included in weather history.

2.4 Preferences and app state

The App stores settings locally, which may include:

  • temperature, pressure, pressure-type, and wind-speed units;
  • display precision and visibility choices;
  • whether the thermometer displays indoor or outdoor temperature;
  • indoor calibration offset;
  • first-launch and rating-prompt state; and
  • a local flag showing that Google Play confirmed or restored Remove Ads.

2.5 Information you choose to share or send

When you use the share feature, the App prepares a text summary containing the displayed location, outdoor weather, available indoor estimate, and time. The summary is sent only to the app or recipient you choose through the operating system's share interface. If you email us for support, we receive the information you include in that message.

3. Network Requests and Third-Party Weather or Map Services

Network requests normally reveal technical information such as your IP address, request time, app or browser identifier, and requested resource to the service operator. Depending on the feature, a request may also include coordinates, a location-search query, or map-tile coordinates.

  • Open-Meteo: primary current-weather, forecast, and location-search service. See Open-Meteo Terms & Privacy.
  • MET Norway: fallback current-weather service when the primary weather request fails. See MET Weather API Terms of Service and MET Norway privacy information.
  • OpenStreetMap: map tiles and Nominatim reverse geocoding. Viewing the map sends requests associated with the visible map area. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts: the App may download interface font resources from Google when they are not already available locally. See Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Advertising

Unless Remove Ads is active, the App may display adaptive banner and full-screen interstitial advertisements through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect or share data such as:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID or app set ID where available;
  • app launches, ad requests, impressions, clicks, and other product interactions;
  • device, operating-system, and app information; and
  • diagnostic, performance, security, and fraud-prevention information.

Google and participating advertising partners may use this information for ad delivery, frequency control, personalization where allowed, measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention. We do not intentionally send your indoor sensor reading, locally saved weather history, or calibration setting to AdMob for ad targeting.

Advertising identifiers and personalization choices can be managed through your device or Google account settings and, where available, through controls shown with an advertisement. Removing ads stops the App from requesting its banner and interstitial advertisements on that installation, but does not require Google to erase previously processed information.

Learn more from Google's Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google Mobile Ads data disclosures.

5. Google Play Billing and Remove Ads

On supported Android installations, the App offers a one-time, non-consumable Remove Ads product through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the transaction and payment method. We do not receive your complete payment-card or bank-account details.

The App receives product information, localized price, purchase status, product identifier, purchase token or verification information, and restoration results needed to start, acknowledge, restore, and deliver the ad-free entitlement. After a successful or restored purchase, a local entitlement flag is stored on the device.

Google processes purchase information under the Google Privacy Policy and Google Play Terms of Service.

6. Permissions

The App may use or request:

  • Location while in use: only when you choose current-location weather or ask the map to center on your current position;
  • Internet access: for weather, forecasts, location search, reverse geocoding, maps, fonts, advertisements, billing, rating links, policy links, and other external content; and
  • Device sensor and battery access: to display an indoor estimate and charging-related accuracy notice. These are platform-provided readings and do not require a runtime permission on supported Android devices.

You can deny or revoke location permission in device settings. Manual-location features remain available without location permission, but live weather, map, advertisements, billing, and external content require an internet connection.

The selected location, preferences, cached reading, history, and Remove Ads flag remain on your device until replaced, cleared through an available App control, removed by clearing App storage, or deleted by uninstalling the App. The History screen provides a control to delete locally saved history.

Information sent to third parties is retained under their policies. Google Play may retain purchase and transaction records for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, refund, and restoration purposes. Clearing App data or uninstalling the App does not erase a Google Play order.
